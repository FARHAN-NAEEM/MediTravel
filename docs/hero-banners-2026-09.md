# Homepage Campaign Banners

Created with the built-in image_gen tool, using public/images/asian-health-connect-logo.png as the brand reference. These are illustrative AI-generated scenes, not photographs of affiliated medical staff or facilities.

The four original PNGs are stored in public/images/hero-banners-2026-09/. BrandedHeroSlidesSeeder installs editable copies on the public storage disk and adds four active slides once. It preserves custom uploads and skips later runs, including after an admin edits or deletes a campaign slide. cPanel deployment runs this installer after migrations. Local installation: php artisan db:seed --class=BrandedHeroSlidesSeeder --force.

All banners use Show full image. The existing-slide migration also switches previous Fill frame slides to Show full image. Admins may explicitly select Fill frame again for photography, but it crops edges. The frame remains 8:5; transparent areas render on white.
